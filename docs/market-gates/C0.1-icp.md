## ICP hypothesis

Local fashion and accessories sellers who:

- sell on Shopee and/or TikTok Shop;
- process approximately 300–3,000 orders/month;
- manage tens to hundreds of active SKUs with size/color/model variants;
- operate with a 2–10 person team;
- experience a mismatch between marketplace turnover, balance, and cash received;
- keep COGS in spreadsheets or have incomplete COGS;
- are affected by returns, cancellations, marketplace fees, and ads fees;
- have outgrown spreadsheets but do not yet want a large ERP.

This is a validation beachhead, not a hard-coded domain boundary.
