## Measurement definitions

- **Time:** elapsed active work time, not calendar delay.
- **Discrepancy:** a source disagreement with a documented reference.
- **COGS coverage:** active SKU count with a verified cost divided by active SKU
  count; estimated and actual must be separate.
- **Time saved:** compare the same workflow before/after, with dates and
  comparable scope.
