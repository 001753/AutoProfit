# C0.1 — Behavioral interview guide

**Status:** READY FOR REVIEW; not an interview record  
**Target:** 15 seller interviews and 5 workflow observations  
**Consent:** obtain before recording identifiable data

## Protocol

1. Ask about the most recent real event, not general opinions.
2. Ask for approximate time, amount, owner, source, and consequence.
3. Request artifacts only with permission; redact PII and credentials.
4. Separate direct observation from participant interpretation.
5. Record uncertainty as unknown; never infer a number.

## Interview questions

1. Walk me through the last time you closed a daily/weekly/monthly report.
2. Which sources did you use, and where did they disagree?
3. When did you last discover that profit or cash received was wrong?
4. How did you reconcile a marketplace settlement to the bank account?
5. How many discrepancies were unresolved, and how long did the last one take?
6. How do you calculate COGS for a variant, return, or damaged item?
7. Which purchase, price, or stock decision was delayed because data was not
   trusted or available?
8. Who performs this work, and what is the handoff?
9. What happens when an order, fee, return, or adjustment cannot be mapped?
10. What did you try before, and why did it fail or stop being used?
11. What measurable outcome would justify changing the current workflow?
12. What data could you safely provide for a pilot, and under what consent?

## Observation checklist

- [ ] Observe a real report/reconciliation workflow.
- [ ] Record start/end time and steps (no secret/PII capture).
- [ ] Record source systems/files and handoffs.
- [ ] Record discrepancy/exception handling.
- [ ] Record decision delayed or enabled.
- [ ] Participant reviewed the notes.
