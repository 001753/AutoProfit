---
name: Roadmap document authority
description: Canonical ownership between the product PRD, operational roadmap, and execution blueprint.
---

The PRD owns product requirements; the execution blueprint owns the canonical
`PXX.Y` breakdown and subphase Definition of Complete; the operational roadmap
owns global sequencing, dependency policy, change control, checkpoints, and
GitHub delivery protocol.

**Why:** Two documents previously exposed the project to premature completion
claims because their subphase granularity differed without an explicit
precedence rule.

**How to apply:** When planning or executing a task, use the blueprint for the
exact subphase and acceptance proof, and use the roadmap for cross-phase
ordering and delivery controls. Treat unavailable external credentials,
approvals, legal review, or payment providers as `BLOCKED`, never as permission
to create a fake integration.
